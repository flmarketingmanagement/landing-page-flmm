Key concepts

E-commerce & websites: key concepts

Conversion rate
The percentage of visits that end in a purchase or the desired action.
Core Web Vitals
Google metrics that measure a page’s loading speed, responsiveness and visual stability.
301 redirect
A permanent signal that a page moved to a new URL; it preserves rankings during a site migration.

FAQ

E-commerce & websites FAQ

Which platform is best for my online store?

It depends on your catalog, budget and team. Shopify is usually the best choice for selling online quickly and reliably; WordPress with WooCommerce offers more content flexibility, and Wix works well for simpler sites. We recommend the platform that best fits your case.

Is the site SEO-ready?

Yes. Every project includes clean architecture, fast load times, metadata, structured data and internal linking, plus a foundation ready to rank on Google and appear in AI-generated answers from the day the site launches. We also hand over the site with Google Search Console and tracking fully configured.

Can you migrate my current store?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and content from other platforms, and set up 301 redirects so you keep the search rankings you already have. Before switching, we test everything in a staging environment to avoid any drop in sales.

What makes an online store convert better?

A store converts better when it loads fast, works well on mobile, shows clear product information, builds trust with visible reviews and policies, and has a simple checkout. We measure every step of the purchase process to find where customers drop off.
